Assumptions
  • Footprint from the approved drawing (plot 268): about 25'3" × 28', roughly 690 sq ft. The brick room with the jasta roof at the south-west is demolished.
  • New rooms, bath and a south balcony (depth switchable in Layers) use the west half. New walls sit on the existing wall lines; the bath sits over the existing toilet so it drains into the same stack.
  • The stair gets an RCC cover with the 1,000 L tank on its slab.
  • The east half is a covered terrace under a jasta shed — salvaged sheets from the old room first, new sheets for the rest — so the leaking slab is fully covered.
  • Owner confirms the frame can take another floor; still get an engineer to check the tank slab and the new wall lines.

Concept only — not a measured survey or permit drawing. Verify dimensions, slab loading, waterproofing and service routes on site.
