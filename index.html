Assumptions
  • Existing rooftop room is demolished; existing staircase entry is kept.
  • Option A: separate office and living room, each with its own door. Options B and C: one open 20 × 10 ft room with a ceiling-track curtain between the desk and the bed — no partition wall.

Concept only — not a measured survey or permit drawing. Verify dimensions, slab loading, waterproofing and service routes on site.
